# AI4S Profile Matching

This profile covers AI for Science (AI4S) research focused on:

1. AI-driven material design and discovery, especially piezoelectric, thermoelectric, and ferroelectric materials and devices.
2. Finite element method (FEM) and phase-field simulation studies of piezoelectric, ferroelectric, and thermoelectric materials and devices.
3. Current research hotspots in piezoelectric, ferroelectric, and thermoelectric materials, including new characterization methods, performance analysis tools, and simulation software.

Content that belongs to this profile:
- Papers on machine learning, deep learning, or AI methods applied to material discovery, property prediction, or device optimization in piezo/thermo/ferroelectric systems.
- Papers on phase-field modeling, finite element simulation, or multiphysics coupling of ferroelectric, piezoelectric, or thermoelectric materials.
- Papers on new characterization techniques, measurement methods, or performance benchmarking for piezo/thermo/ferroelectric materials.
- Software, tools, or datasets for computational materials science relevant to piezo/thermo/ferroelectric research.

Content that does NOT belong:
- General AI news, LLM releases, or AI tool announcements without materials science focus.
- Pure software engineering or general computing papers without materials science application.
- General condensed matter physics not specifically about piezo/thermo/ferroelectric materials.
- General chemistry or materials science papers not related to piezo/thermo/ferroelectric or computational simulation.
